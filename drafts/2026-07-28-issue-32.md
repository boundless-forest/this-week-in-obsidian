### 💎 Gem of the Week
[Memio](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/memio/dgmbleogimlcioecnohcpfbbcpjafeia) is a free, no-account browser extension that saves highlighted text and notes straight into your vault while you read, no copy-paste-and-tab-switch routine. It writes to Obsidian, Notion, and Todoist from the same extension, so a highlight worth keeping lands where you already work. Also available all Chromium based browsers and Firefox.
